# TJ Viola website
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
A complete artist and session musician website based on TJ Viola’s public Instagram, YouTube, and linked official sources.
## Users
Listeners exploring original music, followed by artists, producers, and promoters seeking session or live work. The user confirmed original music first, followed by performances and booking.
## Stack
Portable static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with locally bundled Three.js. `dist/` serves directly with no build step. No backend or additional runtime dependencies are needed for platform playback and external booking through SoundBetter. Deployment defaults to private.
## Brand Commitments
Use shades of blue, with vibrant glowing cobalt/cyan light, a continuous animated environment, and fluid organic forms instead of opaque rectangular section bands. Use elegant uppercase typography; heavy block lettering is rejected. TJ Forma supplies thin geometric uppercase lettering throughout, with a transparent vector tracing of the supplied TJ/VIOLA logo. TJ Forma is a renamed OFL derivative of Gruppo with custom reference-inspired capitals; the screenshot’s original font is not identified. Preserve real artist imagery and sourced credits. Give the organic perimeter of every image a broad, gradual blurred gradient fade into the background while retaining crisp interiors. Use upgraded source photographs and responsive derivatives without artificial enlargement or a universal 4K claim. Continue without Runway; generation was blocked by insufficient credits.

Upgraded hero source at 3700 × 2467, portraits at 1440 × 1920 and 1600 × 1143, release artwork up to 3000 pixels square, and 22 video thumbnails at 1280 × 720.
